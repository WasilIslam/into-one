How it works

Connect
1

Add any number of Gmail accounts and Slack workspaces. A sign-in window opens, you click Allow. No keys.

Ask
2

Type “read last 6 hours”. It reads whole conversations, including your own replies, up to 24 hours back.

Act
3

Tick tasks off, ask about any of them, and send the reply from the same window.
